Here is a structured summary of the Q3 2025 Business Review:

- **Period:** Q3 2025 (July – September 2025), report dated October 8, 2025
- **Revenue:** $4.7M total, up 12% year-over-year, exceeding the $4.4M forecast

  - SMB segment: $1.8M, missed $2.1M target by 14%

- **Product:** 14 feature releases shipped; onboarding time-to-value improved from 11 days to 6 days
- **Support:** Ticket volume down 8% QoQ; average response time 3.2 hours (within 4-hour target)
- **Key Risks:** Cloud cost overruns threatening Q4 margin targets; SMB pipeline coverage at 1.8x (below 2.5x threshold); a $340K enterprise renewal at risk due to competitor evaluation
- **Priority Actions:** SMB win-back campaign (Nov 1), cloud cost optimization sprint (Q4), proactive churn health scoring for SMB accounts (Nov 15), pricing scenario modeling (Oct 22)
